### 4.1 "Crawled – currently not indexed" (256 pages)

**Likely cause:** These are company pages with thin content (<2 financial statements) or pages Google considers low-quality. Our `noindex` quality gate should handle most of these, but 256 pages were crawled before the gate was applied or are pages that pass the gate but Google still considers thin.

**Action:** After deploy, monitor whether this number grows. If `/firma/` pages start appearing here in large numbers, it means Google considers them thin — we may need to raise the quality gate from `fsCount >= 2` to `fsCount >= 3`.

### 4.2 "Alternate page with proper canonical" (189) + "Duplicate, different canonical" (25)

**Likely cause:** Hreflang variants (`/en/firma/...`, `/de/firma/...`) being treated as duplicates of the SK version. This is **expected behavior** — Google correctly identifies the SK version as canonical and the localized versions as alternates. The 189 "alternate with proper canonical" means Google IS respecting our canonical tags.

**The 25 "duplicate, different canonical"** is more concerning — Google chose a different canonical than we specified. This could be:
- Company pages without slug (`/firma/35955678`) vs with slug (`/firma/35955678-soitron-s-r-o`) — Google may be choosing one over the other
- Old `/odvetvie/` vs new `/firmy/` — Google may not have processed the redirect yet

**Action:** After deploy, check if these numbers decrease. The 308 redirects should resolve the `/odvetvie/` duplicates.

### 4.3 "Duplicate without canonical" (73)

**Likely cause:** Pages that have no canonical tag and Google considers duplicates. These could be:
- Screener URL variants without canonical (`/screener?page=2&sort=trzby`)
- `/firmy` filter variants without canonical

**Action:** Verify that all indexable pages have self-canonical. The regression tests check this.

## 6. SEO Opportunities (ranked by value)

### Opportunity 1: ORSR glossary page — title/meta optimization

**Current:** `/slovnik/orsr` ranks at position 7.3 for "orsr" with 16,112 impressions and 0.06% CTR (10 clicks).

**Potential:** If CTR improved to 2% (typical for position 1–3), that's **320 clicks/month**. If position improved to 3, CTR could be 3–5% → **480–800 clicks/month**.

**Action:** Optimize title and meta description for `/slovnik/orsr`. Current title likely says "ORSR — Slovník | Verifa.sk". Better: "ORSR — Obchodný register SR: čo je a ako ho využiť | Verifa.sk". Make it clear this is a useful resource, not just a dictionary entry.

**Effort:** 30 minutes. **Value:** High (320–800 clicks/month potential).

### Opportunity 2: Company page title/meta optimization

**Current:** 822 company pages get 5,516 impressions and 76 clicks (1.38% CTR). 147 pages have >10 impressions and 0 clicks.

**Pattern:** Company pages at position 6–9 with 20–40 impressions and 0 clicks. Users see Verifa in results but don't click.

**Action:** Improve company page title/meta description. Current title likely: "Firma name — IČO | Verifa.sk". Better: "Firma name — finančné údaje, riziká, IČO {ico} | Verifa.sk". Show value in the snippet — "finančné údaje" and "riziká" signal useful content.

**Effort:** 2–4 hours (template change). **Value:** High — if CTR doubles from 1.38% to 2.76%, that's **76 → 152 clicks/month**.

### Opportunity 3: Company pages without slug — 308 redirect consolidation

**Current:** Pages like `/firma/35955678` (no slug) and `/firma/35955678-soitron-s-r-o` (with slug) both exist in GSC. The no-slug version gets 24 impressions, 1 click. The with-slug version gets 26 impressions, 0 clicks.

**Issue:** Google is splitting link equity and impressions across two URLs for the same company. This contributes to the 25 "duplicate, different canonical" issues.

**Action:** The middleware already 308-redirects `/firma/{ico}` (no slug) → `/firma/{ico}-{slug}`. Verify this is working for all cases and that the no-slug URLs are dropping from GSC.

**Effort:** Already implemented. **Value:** Medium — consolidates link equity.

### Opportunity 4: City hub pages (`/mesto/`) — highest CTR hub type

**Current:** 24 city pages get 139 impressions, 3 clicks, **2.16% CTR** — the highest CTR of any hub type. Average position 11.5 (page 2).

**Insight:** Users search by city name ("firmy v Kolárove", "firmy Prakovce"). City hubs have higher commercial intent than NACE hubs.

**Action:** After `/firmy/` hubs are indexed, consider adding NACE × city to sitemap with quality gate (≥20 firms). City-level pages have proven CTR.

**Effort:** P2.1 (deferred). **Value:** Medium-high — 2.16% CTR is promising.

### Opportunity 5: English-language due diligence queries

**Current:** "due diligence companies" (19 impr, pos 52), "international financial due diligence" (10 impr, pos 71). USA + UK = 1,271 impressions, 0 clicks.

**Issue:** Verifa has English company pages (`/en/firma/...`) but they rank too low for English commercial queries.

**Action:** Create an English landing page targeting "due diligence Slovakia" or "company verification Slovakia". This is a P2 business-intent page.

**Effort:** P2 (deferred). **Value:** Medium — 1,271 impressions from US/UK with 0 clicks. Low-hanging fruit if we create targeted English content.

## 7. Recommended Next Development Task

### Recommendation: Company page title/meta optimization (Opportunity 2)

**Why this first:**
1. **Highest ROI** — 822 company pages already get 5,516 impressions. Doubling CTR = +76 clicks/month with zero new pages.
2. **Affects existing traffic** — doesn't depend on Google indexing new URLs.
3. **Fast to implement** — template change in `generateMetadata` for company pages.
4. **Measurable in 1–2 weeks** — GSC updates quickly for title/meta changes on already-indexed pages.
5. **Doesn't require new architecture** — no new routes, no sitemap changes, no redirects.

**What to change:**
- Company page `<title>`: Include financial keywords ("finančné údaje", "riziká", "IČO")
- Company page `<meta description>`: Show value proposition ("Finančné údaje, rizikové signály a registrácia firmy {name} — IČO {ico}. Overenie z verejných registrov SR.")
- Test with 10–20 high-impression companies first (RPC Bramlage, Firstin, Fach Centrum, Prosight, Soitron, CEVA, Kofola, Handlopex)

**What NOT to do yet:**
- ❌ Do not create NACE × city pages (P2.1)
- ❌ Do not create business-intent pages (P2.2)
- ❌ Do not change URL architecture
- ❌ Do not add new pages to sitemap

### Secondary recommendation: ORSR glossary title optimization (Opportunity 1)

**Why:** 16,112 impressions at 0.06% CTR is the single biggest quick-win. One page, one title change, potential 320–800 clicks/month.

**What to change:**
- `/slovnik/orsr` title: "ORSR — Obchodný register SR: čo je a ako ho využiť | Verifa.sk"
